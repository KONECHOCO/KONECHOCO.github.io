Reperibilità Planner Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 18, 2026

English

Accounts and company data. To use the full app you create an account with your email and a password. The data you enter (company details, operators, shifts, sensor maps, intervention logs) is stored in the cloud (Google Firebase, EU region) and is visible only to members of your own company. Companies cannot see each other's data. You can delete your account and, if you are the last member, all company data from the Account screen inside the app. The Demo mode uses generic data stored only on your device.

Reperibilità Planner is an on-call scheduling and sensor-map tool. We do not require an account and do not intentionally collect names, contacts, precise location or user-created content.

To show advertising the app uses Unity Ads, which may process limited technical information such as device identifiers, IP address, approximate location derived from IP, diagnostics and ad interaction data, subject to Unity's privacy policy. You can manage advertising and tracking permissions in your device settings.

Data entered in the app (shifts, operators, logs) are stored locally on your device. We do not sell personal information. Contact: konechoco@gmail.com
