FAQ

Where do prices come from? / Da dove vengono i prezzi?
Official government sources: MIMIT (Italy), prix-carburants.gouv.fr (France), MITECO (Spain), Tankerkönig / MTS-K (Germany). Stations are required by law to report their prices. Every price shows when it was last updated.

A price looks wrong. / Un prezzo sembra sbagliato.
The price is the one the station reported to the authority. Prices not updated for more than 3 days and unusually low prices are flagged in the app. You can report the real price with "Update price".

How do I remove ads? / Come tolgo la pubblicità?
Buy FuelSaver Pro (one-time purchase, no subscription) from the PRO button. Already bought it on another device? Open PRO → "Restore purchases".
