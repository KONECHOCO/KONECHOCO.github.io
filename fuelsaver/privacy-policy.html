Privacy Policy — FuelSaver

FuelSaver: Prezzi Carburanti · Last updated: September 27, 2026 · Contact: konechocoproduction@gmail.com

1. Location

If you allow it, the app reads your approximate or precise location to show the fuel stations around you. The coordinates are sent to our server (fuel.ikonetsolutions.com) only to search the stations within the radius you chose; they are not stored, not linked to any identifier and not shared with anyone. You can also type a city instead of using GPS. When you search a place or calculate a trip, the text you type is sent to OpenStreetMap Nominatim and OSRM to find the place and the road distance.

2. Fuel prices

Prices come from official public sources: the Italian Ministry of Enterprises (MIMIT – Osservaprezzi Carburanti), the French government (prix-carburants.gouv.fr), the Spanish Ministry for the Ecological Transition (MITECO) and, for Germany, Tankerkönig / MTS-K (CC BY 4.0). No personal data is involved.

3. What stays on your phone

Price reports and reviews you write, your settings and the "unlocked for 24 hours" state of premium features are saved only on your device. We do not receive them.

4. Advertising

The free version shows ads served by Google AdMob. Google may collect device identifiers (such as the advertising identifier / IDFA), approximate location derived from the IP address and interaction data to show and measure ads. In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland the app asks for your consent through Google's certified consent form before showing personalised ads; on iOS it also asks for permission to track through Apple's App Tracking Transparency. If you refuse, you may still see non-personalised ads. You can change your choice at any time from the device settings. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

5. Purchases

FuelSaver Pro is a one-time in-app purchase handled entirely by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play). We never see your payment details; the app only checks with the store whether you own FuelSaver Pro. Pro removes all ads.

6. Children

The app is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their data.

7. Your rights

Since we do not store personal data on our servers, there is nothing to access or delete on our side. For data processed by Google for advertising, see Google's privacy policy. For any question write to konechocoproduction@gmail.com.

Informativa sulla privacy — FuelSaver

1. Posizione

Se lo consenti, l'app legge la tua posizione per mostrarti i distributori vicini. Le coordinate vengono inviate al nostro server (fuel.ikonetsolutions.com) solo per cercare i distributori nel raggio scelto: non vengono salvate né associate a identificativi né condivise. Puoi anche digitare una città. Il testo delle ricerche di luoghi e il calcolo del percorso vengono inviati a OpenStreetMap Nominatim e OSRM.

2. Prezzi

I prezzi provengono da fonti pubbliche ufficiali: MIMIT – Osservaprezzi Carburanti (Italia), prix-carburants.gouv.fr (Francia), MITECO (Spagna), Tankerkönig / MTS-K (Germania).

3. Dati sul telefono

Segnalazioni di prezzo, recensioni e impostazioni restano solo sul tuo dispositivo.

4. Pubblicità

La versione gratuita mostra annunci Google AdMob, che può raccogliere identificativi del dispositivo (es. IDFA), posizione approssimativa dall'indirizzo IP e dati di interazione. Nello Spazio Economico Europeo l'app chiede il consenso tramite il modulo certificato di Google e, su iOS, il permesso di tracciamento (App Tracking Transparency). Puoi cambiare scelta in qualsiasi momento.

5. Acquisti

FuelSaver Pro è un acquisto una tantum gestito da Apple o Google: non vediamo mai i tuoi dati di pagamento. Pro rimuove tutta la pubblicità.
