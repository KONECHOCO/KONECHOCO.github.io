1. The service

FuelSaver shows fuel prices published by official public sources (MIMIT – Osservaprezzi Carburanti in Italy, prix-carburants.gouv.fr in France, MITECO in Spain, Tankerkönig / MTS-K in Germany) and helps you find and reach nearby stations. The app is provided "as is".

2. Accuracy of prices

Prices are communicated by station operators to the authorities and may be late, wrong or changed at the pump. The app shows when each price was last updated and flags outdated or unusual prices, but the price you pay is always the one displayed at the station. We are not responsible for differences between the app and the pump.

3. Driving safety

Do not use the app while driving. Always follow the road rules and the instructions of your navigation app.

4. Your contributions

Price reports and reviews you write are stored only on your device. Do not enter offensive, false or personal information.

5. Advertising and FuelSaver Pro

The free version contains ads and some features can be unlocked for 24 hours by watching a rewarded video. You can restore Pro on a new device with the same store account.
