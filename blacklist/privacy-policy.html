Privacy Policy — Blacklist Call Blocker

Blacklist Call Blocker · Last updated: September 25, 2026 · Contact: konechocoproduction@gmail.com

1. What stays on your phone

Your personal lists (blocked numbers, ranges, allowed numbers), your settings and, on Android, the log of screened calls are stored only on your device. We never read your contacts, your call history or your messages. On Android, call screening runs entirely on the device against the downloaded list: incoming calls are never sent to us.

2. What we receive

  • Reports you send. When you tap "Report", we receive the reported phone number, the category you picked, the optional short description you typed, the country set in the app, and a random installation identifier (generated on the device, not linked to your name, phone number, Apple ID or advertising ID). It lets us count each installation only once per number. We also keep the IP address of the request for up to 30 days, only to limit abuse.
  • Lookups. When you look up a number, we receive that number and your country, to return its community reports. Lookups are not stored with any identifier.
  • List downloads. The app periodically downloads the community list of your country. The request contains only the country code.

A number is added to the shared list only after reports from several different installations. Descriptions are shown to other users without any information about who wrote them.

3. Your number was reported by mistake?

Anyone can ask for a number to be removed at blacklist.ikonetsolutions.com/remove. We review every request and reply by email.

4. Advertising

The app is free and shows non-personalized ads: full-screen videos from Unity Ads (Unity Technologies) and a banner from Google AdMob. The app does not ask for App Tracking Transparency permission and does not track you across other companies' apps or websites. In the EEA and UK we ask for consent with Google's certified consent form. These SDKs may process device type, language and country, approximate location derived from the IP address, ad views and clicks and diagnostic data. They never access your lists or calls.

5. "Remove ads" purchase

A one-time in-app purchase removes ads. Payment is handled entirely by Apple or Google: we never receive payment details.

6. Retention and your rights

Reports are kept while they are useful to protect users and are removed on a valid removal request. Since reports are tied only to a random identifier, write to us with the number concerned to exercise your rights of access, rectification or erasure under the GDPR. Deleting the app deletes everything stored on your phone.

7. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13.

8. Changes

We will update this page if the app's data practices change. The date at the top is the current version.
