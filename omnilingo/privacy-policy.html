Privacy Policy — OmniLingo

OmniLingo: Offline Translator · Last updated: October 14, 2026 · Contact: konechocoproduction@gmail.com

1. Translation and text recognition

Text translation, language detection and recognition of text in photos are performed on your device with Google ML Kit. The texts you translate and the photos you scan are not sent to our servers. The first time you use a language, the app downloads its language model (about 30 MB) from Google; this download does not contain your texts.

2. Voice

Voice dictation, face-to-face conversation and live subtitles use the speech recognition service of your operating system (Apple Speech on iOS, Google speech services on Android). Depending on your device and settings, these services may process the audio on Apple's or Google's servers under their own privacy policies. Read-aloud uses the system text-to-speech voices. We do not record or store audio.

3. Camera, photos and documents

The camera and the photo library are used only when you choose to scan an image. Documents you open (PDF, Word, text files) are read on the device. For some alphabets that are not supported offline (for example Arabic, Cyrillic, Greek, Hebrew or Thai), the app downloads an open-source recognition model the first time; the image itself is still processed on the device.

4. What stays on your phone

Your translation history, flashcards, glossary and settings are saved only on your device. You can export them as a backup file or delete them from Settings. We do not receive them.

5. Advertising

The free version shows ads served by Google AdMob. Google may collect device identifiers (such as the advertising identifier / IDFA), approximate location derived from the IP address and interaction data to show and measure ads. In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland the app asks for your consent through Google's certified consent form before showing personalised ads; on iOS it also asks for permission to track through Apple's App Tracking Transparency. If you refuse, you may still see non-personalised ads. You can change your choice at any time from the device settings. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

6. Purchases

OmniLingo Pro is a one-time in-app purchase handled entirely by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play). We never see your payment details; the app only checks with the store whether you own OmniLingo Pro. Pro removes all ads.

7. Children

The app is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their data.

8. Your rights

Since we do not store personal data on our servers, there is nothing to access or delete on our side. For data processed by Google or Apple, see their privacy policies. For any question write to konechocoproduction@gmail.com.

Informativa sulla privacy — OmniLingo

1. Traduzione e riconoscimento del testo

La traduzione, il riconoscimento della lingua e del testo nelle foto avvengono sul dispositivo con Google ML Kit. I testi tradotti e le foto non vengono inviati ai nostri server. Al primo uso di una lingua l'app scarica da Google il relativo modello (circa 30 MB), che non contiene i tuoi testi.

2. Voce

Dettatura, conversazione faccia a faccia e sottotitoli live usano il riconoscimento vocale del sistema operativo (Apple su iOS, Google su Android), che a seconda del dispositivo può elaborare l'audio sui server di Apple o Google secondo le loro informative. Non registriamo né conserviamo audio.

3. Fotocamera, foto e documenti

Fotocamera e foto sono usate solo quando scegli di scansionare un'immagine. I documenti aperti sono letti sul dispositivo. Per alcuni alfabeti non supportati offline l'app scarica al primo uso un modello open source; l'immagine resta comunque sul dispositivo.

4. Dati sul telefono

Cronologia, flashcard, glossario e impostazioni restano solo sul tuo dispositivo. Puoi esportarli o eliminarli dalle Impostazioni.

5. Pubblicità

La versione gratuita mostra annunci Google AdMob, che può raccogliere identificativi del dispositivo (es. IDFA), posizione approssimativa dall'indirizzo IP e dati di interazione. Nello Spazio Economico Europeo l'app chiede il consenso tramite il modulo certificato di Google e, su iOS, il permesso di tracciamento (App Tracking Transparency). Puoi cambiare scelta in qualsiasi momento.

6. Acquisti

OmniLingo Pro è un acquisto una tantum gestito da Apple o Google: non vediamo mai i tuoi dati di pagamento. Pro rimuove tutta la pubblicità.
