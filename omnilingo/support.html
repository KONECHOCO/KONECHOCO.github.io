Frequently asked questions

How do I translate offline? Open Settings → Offline language packs and download the languages you need (about 30 MB each) while connected, preferably on Wi-Fi.

Voice recognition does not work. Allow microphone and speech recognition access in your device settings. Some languages may not be supported by your device's speech service.

A PDF shows no text. Scanned PDFs contain only images: use "Photos & camera" to recognize the text.

How do I restore OmniLingo Pro? Open the Pro screen and tap "Restore purchases" while signed in with the same Apple ID or Google account.
